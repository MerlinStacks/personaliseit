OverCustomise 1.19.5 - 12 October 2026

Provides a compatibility ZIP rooted at personaliseit-main for manual replacement of existing GitHub source-ZIP installations. Includes the SLDevs-hosted updater introduced in 1.19.4; future hosted updates preserve the existing plugin folder.

Upload the compatibility ZIP through WordPress and choose to replace the existing plugin. Do not uninstall first. No database schema change is introduced by this release.

Includes all 1.19.4 features and its upload retention policy: unused uploads expire after 48 hours, or after 7 days when still in an active or saved cart. Order-linked artwork is retained.

Requires WordPress 6.8+, PHP 8.2+, and WooCommerce. Standard overcustomise ZIPs remain the packages used by the hosted update feed.
